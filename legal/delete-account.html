Delete your account and data

This page explains how to request deletion of your AI Draftsman B2B account, what is removed when you do, and what we are obliged to keep.

Who this applies to

AI Draftsman B2B is a workspace tool for businesses. Accounts are created by a workspace owner or administrator, not by signing up in the app, so a request to delete an account usually comes from the person who holds it or from the workspace owner acting on their behalf.

How to request deletion

In the app

Open Settings → Account and choose Delete account. Confirming raises a deletion request against your account and we handle it from there.

By email

Write to info@aidraftsman.ai from the address your account uses, with the subject Account deletion request. Sending from the registered address is how we confirm the request is genuinely yours.

What is deleted

Once a request is verified, the following are permanently removed:

  • Your profile — name, email address and profile photo
  • Your sign-in credentials, including your password and two-factor authenticator enrolment
  • Your workspace membership, role and permissions
  • Chat conversations you started, and the messages and attachments in them
  • Support tickets you raised and any replies or attachments you added
  • Notifications addressed to you

What stays, and why

Some records are not yours alone to remove, and some we are required to keep.

  • Workspace data — customers, leads, agents, knowledge bases, API keys and reports belong to the business that owns the workspace, not to an individual member. Deleting your account does not delete them. See the section below if the whole workspace should go.
  • Billing and usage records — invoices, credit transactions and consumption figures are kept to meet accounting and tax obligations.
  • Security and audit records — a minimal record of significant actions is retained so the workspace owner keeps an accurate audit trail. Where possible these are de-identified.

Anything retained is kept only for as long as the relevant legal or contractual obligation requires, then deleted on our normal retention schedule.

How long it takes

We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days. Backup copies are overwritten on their own cycle and are fully cleared within 90 days of the request. You will receive confirmation by email once your account has been removed.

Deleting an entire workspace

If a business is closing its account altogether, the workspace owner should email info@aidraftsman.ai from the owner's registered address. This removes every member account, all agents, customers, leads, knowledge bases and conversations belonging to that workspace, subject to the retention rules above.

Changed your mind

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone once it has been carried out. If you contact us before the request has been processed, we can cancel it.

Questions about your data?

Email info@aidraftsman.ai and we will respond within five working days. If you are in the UK or EU, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.
